
  Down the lane the chowkidar counts the hours
  by how often the dogs decide who they are.
  A motorcycle stitches the dark and is gone,
  leaving behind the smell of somebody's errand.

  I am awake for no good reason except
  that the day was loud and I want to hear
  what it sounds like after everyone has stopped
  explaining themselves to it.

  The fan turns. The city exhales.
  Tomorrow will arrive already halfway through
  its own sentence, and I will nod
  like I have been listening the whole time.

⏺ Inventory

  Three chargers, none of them the right one.
  A hoodie that belongs to a version of me
  who ran in the mornings. A receipt
  for a coffee I do not remember drinking
  in a city I remember perfectly.

  The drawer keeps things the way a friend does:
  without judgment, and without a system.
  Here is a key to a door that was repainted.
  Here is a battery that may or may not be dead.
  The only way to find out is to need it.

  I used to think a life was what you chose.
  Now I think it is mostly what you kept
  when moving, when the boxes ran out
  and the taxi was already downstairs honking,
  and your hand went to one thing and not another.

  So: the key stays. The receipt stays.
  The hoodie I will wear to sleep in
  until it becomes a different kind of promise,
  one I can actually keep.
