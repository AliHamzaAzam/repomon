  ### README.md Summary

  repomon is a mission-control desktop app and TUI for managing and steering fleets of AI coding agents across multiple repositories and worktrees simultaneously.
  Powered by a Rust background daemon (repomond), it offers durable sessions via tmux/ConPTY, integrated Git explorer and editor tooling, and cross-agent fleet communication.
